02 — Flagship

Aurora Ops — an AI agent console for real machines.

◆ Agentic AI

Diagnose → reason → remediate

A fleet of specialist LLM agents — health, logs, backup, auto-remediation — sit behind an orchestrator that hears a symptom and routes it to the right one. Each agent runs its own tools on your servers, then reports back in plain language. You watch the reasoning; you read the verdict.
